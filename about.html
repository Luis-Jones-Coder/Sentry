Meet the Founder

I've Always Been
Fascinated by Business

Photograph coming soon

Long before I knew business consultancy was a profession, I naturally found myself analysing the businesses around me. Whether I was working within an organisation, speaking with business owners or simply experiencing a company as a customer, I would notice opportunities for improvement, identify inefficiencies and think about how things could work better.

Over the years I've continued to develop that curiosity through education, professional experience and continuous learning, building knowledge across business operations, people management, customer experience and organisational improvement.

I've worked across a range of industries including retail, hospitality, logistics, security, recruitment, customer service and HR placements, giving me valuable insight into how different organisations operate and the challenges they face.

Sentry was created because I wanted to turn that passion into something meaningful. My goal isn't simply to provide advice — it's to become a trusted partner that businesses can rely on.

I believe every business deserves someone who genuinely cares about its success, someone who listens, challenges ideas, identifies opportunities and works alongside business owners to help them achieve their goals.

At Sentry, every conversation starts with understanding your business, because the best solutions begin by listening.

Our Mission

Helping Businesses Move Forward With Confidence

Our mission is to help businesses build stronger foundations, overcome challenges and unlock their full potential through practical guidance, honest advice and genuine partnership. We're committed to supporting business owners with solutions that create meaningful improvements, strengthen performance and prepare businesses for sustainable long-term success.

Our Vision

Building One of the UK's Most Trusted Business Consultancies

We want Sentry to become recognised not simply for providing consultancy, but for becoming a trusted partner that businesses rely on throughout their journey. Our vision is to build lasting relationships with ambitious businesses, helping them grow through continuous improvement, stronger operations and practical support that creates real value.
